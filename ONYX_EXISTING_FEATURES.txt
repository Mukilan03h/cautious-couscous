              ONYX PLATFORM - EXISTING FEATURES (AS-IS)
================================================================================
OVERVIEW
================================================================================

ESA is an open-source Enterprise AI Knowledge Platform that enables natural 
language search and chat over company documents with cited answers.

1.1 AI-POWERED SEARCH & CHAT
-----------------------------

   - Multi-turn conversations with context
   - Cited answers with source references
   - Streaming real-time responses
   - Chat sessions and history
   - Feedback (like/dislike) on answers
