OVERVIEW
================================================================================

We are building an Enterprise AI Knowledge Platform that enables employees to
search and chat with company knowledge using natural language. The platform
provides accurate, cited answers from internal documents while maintaining
enterprise-grade security and compliance.

Key Value Proposition:
- Employees get instant answers from company knowledge
- AI provides citations/sources for every answer
- Enterprise security with role-based access control
- Works with 45+ data sources your company already uses
- Self-hosted = full data privacy, no vendor lock-in
